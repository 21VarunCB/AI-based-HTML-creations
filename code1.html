THE "DARK SILICON" PRINCIPLE

Why Specialized Accelerators Beat General-Purpose CPUs

During standby, the big 4.3 GHz Prime cores are completely power-gated (0V) to eliminate subthreshold leakage. Only ultra-low-leakage Efficiency cores wake periodically to handle push notifications.

STAGE 01 // TETRAPRISM 120MM TELEPHOTO Latency: Speed of Light

4× Internal Light Reflections Create a 120mm Focal Length in 8mm

Traditional telephoto lenses require physical distance between the front element and the sensor. By bouncing light four times inside a precision glass tetraprism, modern flagships achieve 5× optical magnification without a protruding camera barrel.

Optical Path = 4 × Folded Prism Reflections → Equivalent 120mm f/2.8 Full-Frame Field of View
